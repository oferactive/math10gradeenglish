Before you start: For absolute value questions, think "distance". For square root questions, write down the domain first and check every answer at the end.

Level A: Basic Questions 1–10

Use the definitions and formulas directly.

  1. Compute.

    1. \(|-7|\)
    2. \(|3-8|\)
    3. \(|2|-|-5|\)
    Show solution

    (a) \(7\)

    (b) \(|-5|=5\)

    (c) \(2-5=-3\)

  2. Find the domain of each function.

    1. \(\sqrt{x-3}\)
    2. \(\dfrac1{x+2}\)
    3. \(\dfrac1{x^2}\)
    4. \(\sqrt{5-x}\)
    Show solution

    (a) \(x\ge3\)

    (b) \(x\ne-2\)

    (c) \(x\ne0\)

    (d) \(x\le5\)

  3. Solve.

    1. \(|x|=4\)
    2. \(|x-2|=5\)
    3. \(|x+1|=0\)
    4. \(|x|=-3\)
    Show solution

    (a) \(x=\pm4\)

    (b) \(x=7\) or \(x=-3\)

    (c) \(x=-1\)

    (d) no solution

  4. For \(f(x)=\tfrac1x\), compute \(f(-2)\), \(f(0.5)\) and \(f(100)\).

    Show solution

    \(-0.5,\ 2,\ 0.01\)

  5. Compute \(\sqrt{49}\), \(\sqrt{0.25}\), and \(\sqrt2\) to three decimal places.

    Show solution

    \(7,\ 0.5,\ 1.414\)

  6. Decide whether each function is even, odd, or neither: \(|x|\), \(\tfrac1x\), \(\tfrac1{x^2}\), \(\sqrt x\).

    Show solution

    \(|x|\): even. \(\tfrac1x\): odd. \(\tfrac1{x^2}\): even. \(\sqrt x\): neither (its domain \(x\ge0\) is not symmetric).

  7. Write down the asymptotes of \(y=\tfrac1x\) and of \(y=\tfrac1{x^2}\).

    Show solution

    Both have the vertical asymptote \(x=0\) and the horizontal asymptote \(y=0\).

  8. Where is \(\tfrac1x\) positive? Where is \(\tfrac1{x^2}\) positive?

    Show solution

    \(\tfrac1x\gt 0\) for \(x\gt 0\). \(\tfrac1{x^2}\gt 0\) for every \(x\ne0\).

  9. Solve the inequalities.

    1. \(|x|\lt 3\)
    2. \(|x|\ge2\)
    Show solution

    (a) \(-3\lt x\lt 3\)

    (b) \(x\le-2\) or \(x\ge2\)

  10. Write \(|x-3|\) without the absolute value sign.

    Show solution

    \(|x-3|=x-3\) when \(x\ge3\), and \(|x-3|=3-x\) when \(x\lt 3\).

Level B: Practice Questions 11–20

Questions with two or three steps, mixing ideas from the unit.

  1. Solve.

    1. \(\sqrt x=5\)
    2. \(\sqrt{x+1}=3\)
    3. \(\sqrt x=-2\)
    Show solution

    (a) \(x=25\)

    (b) \(x+1=9\Rightarrow x=8\)

    (c) no solution, because a square root is never negative

  2. Solve \(|2x-1|=7\).

    Show solution

    \(2x-1=7\Rightarrow x=4\), or \(2x-1=-7\Rightarrow x=-3\).

  3. Solve \(|x-1|\lt 4\) and explain the answer in terms of distance.

    Show solution

    \(-4\lt x-1\lt 4\Rightarrow -3\lt x\lt 5\).

    These are the numbers whose distance from 1 is less than 4.

  4. Find the average rate of change of \(\sqrt x\) on \([0,1]\), \([1,4]\) and \([4,9]\). What do you notice?

    Show solution

    \(\dfrac{1-0}{1}=1\), \(\dfrac{2-1}{3}=\dfrac13\), \(\dfrac{3-2}{5}=\dfrac15\).

    The rate gets smaller: \(\sqrt x\) keeps increasing, but more and more slowly.

  5. Find the average rate of change of \(\tfrac1x\) on \([1,2]\) and on \([2,4]\).

    Show solution

    \(\dfrac{0.5-1}{1}=-0.5\) and \(\dfrac{0.25-0.5}{2}=-0.125\).

    Both are negative (the function decreases) and the second is closer to 0 (it flattens out).

  6. Solve.

    1. \(\tfrac1x=4\)
    2. \(\tfrac1{x^2}=9\)
    3. \(\tfrac1x=x\)
    Show solution

    (a) \(x=\tfrac14\)

    (b) \(x^2=\tfrac19\Rightarrow x=\pm\tfrac13\)

    (c) \(x^2=1\Rightarrow x=\pm1\)

  7. Find where \(y=|x|\) meets \(y=\tfrac12x+3\).

    Show solution

    For \(x\ge0\): \(x=\tfrac12x+3\Rightarrow x=6\), point \((6,6)\).

    For \(x\lt 0\): \(-x=\tfrac12x+3\Rightarrow x=-2\), point \((-2,2)\).

  8. Find where \(y=\sqrt x\) meets \(y=x\). For which \(x\) is \(\sqrt x\gt x\)?

    Show solution

    \(\sqrt x=x\Rightarrow x=x^2\Rightarrow x=0\) or \(x=1\). Points \((0,0)\) and \((1,1)\).

    \(\sqrt x\gt x\) for \(0\lt x\lt 1\) (try \(x=0.25\): \(0.5\gt 0.25\)).

  9. For which \(x\) is \(\tfrac1{x^2}\gt \tfrac1x\)?

    Show solution

    Multiply by \(x^2\gt 0\): \(1\gt x\).

    Answer: \(x\lt 0\) or \(0\lt x\lt 1\).

  10. Is \(f(x)=\tfrac1x\) a decreasing function? Explain.

    Show solution

    It decreases on \(x\lt 0\) and on \(x\gt 0\). But \(-1\lt 1\) and \(f(-1)=-1\lt f(1)=1\), so it is not decreasing on its whole domain.

Level C: Exam level Questions 21–30

Longer questions in the style of the matriculation (bagrut) exam.

  1. Solve \(|x-3|=2x\).

    Show solution

    Case \(x\ge3\): \(x-3=2x\Rightarrow x=-3\), not in this case.

    Case \(x\lt 3\): \(3-x=2x\Rightarrow x=1\) ✓.

    Check: \(|1-3|=2=2\cdot1\) ✓. Answer: \(x=1\).

  2. Solve \(|x+2|+|x-4|=10\), and explain the result in terms of distances.

    Show solution

    \(x\ge4\): \(2x-2=10\Rightarrow x=6\).

    \(-2\le x\lt 4\): \((x+2)+(4-x)=6\ne10\), no solution.

    \(x\lt -2\): \(-2x+2=10\Rightarrow x=-4\).

    Answer: \(x=-4\) or \(x=6\): the points whose distances from \(-2\) and from \(4\) add up to 10.

  3. Solve \(\sqrt{x+6}=x\).

    Show solution

    \(x+6=x^2\Rightarrow x=3\) or \(x=-2\).

    Check: \(x=3\) ✓, \(x=-2\) gives \(2\ne-2\) ✗. Answer: \(x=3\).

  4. Consider \(f(x)=\sqrt x\) and \(g(x)=\tfrac1x\) for \(x\gt 0\).

    1. Find where the graphs meet.
    2. For which \(x\) is \(f(x)\gt g(x)\)?
    Show solution

    (a) \(\sqrt x=\tfrac1x\Rightarrow x\sqrt x=1\Rightarrow x=1\). Point \((1,1)\).

    (b) For \(x\gt 1\): \(\sqrt x\gt 1\gt \tfrac1x\). For \(0\lt x\lt 1\): \(\sqrt x\lt 1\lt \tfrac1x\). So \(f\gt g\) for \(x\gt 1\).

  5. Find the area of the region between \(y=|x|\) and \(y=4\).

    Show solution

    The line \(y=4\) meets \(y=|x|\) at \((-4,4)\) and \((4,4)\).

    The region is a triangle with base 8 and height 4: area \(=\dfrac{8\cdot4}{2}=16\).

  6. \(P\) is a point on the graph of \(y=\tfrac1x\) with \(x\gt 0\). A rectangle has corners \(O(0,0)\), \((x,0)\), \(P\) and \((0,\tfrac1x)\). Show that its area does not depend on where \(P\) is.

    Show solution

    Width \(x\), height \(\tfrac1x\): area \(=x\cdot\tfrac1x=1\), for every \(x\gt 0\).

  7. For which \(x\) is \(\tfrac1{x^2}\gt 100\)?

    Show solution

    For \(x\ne0\): \(x^2\lt \tfrac1{100}\Rightarrow -0.1\lt x\lt 0.1\).

    Answer: \(-0.1\lt x\lt 0.1,\ x\ne0\).

  8. Solve the inequalities.

    1. \(\sqrt x\le3\)
    2. \(\sqrt{x-2}\gt 1\)
    Show solution

    (a) \(0\le x\le9\) (do not forget the domain \(x\ge0\)).

    (b) Domain \(x\ge2\). \(x-2\gt 1\Rightarrow x\gt 3\).

  9. Solve \(|x^2-4|=3\).

    Show solution

    \(x^2-4=3\Rightarrow x=\pm\sqrt7\), or \(x^2-4=-3\Rightarrow x=\pm1\).

    Four solutions: \(\pm1,\ \pm\sqrt7\).

  10. Find every number whose distance from 5 is twice its distance from 2.

    Show solution

    \(|x-5|=2|x-2|\)

    \(x-5=2(x-2)\Rightarrow x=-1\), or \(x-5=-2(x-2)\Rightarrow 3x=9\Rightarrow x=3\).

    Check: \(x=-1\): \(6=2\cdot3\) ✓; \(x=3\): \(2=2\cdot1\) ✓.
