Before you start: Always factor \(f'(x)\) before making the sign table. One test value in each interval is enough.

Level A: Basic Questions 1–10

Use the definitions and formulas directly.

  1. Find the intervals of increase and decrease and the extreme point of \(f(x)=x^2-4x+3\).

    Show solution

    \(f'(x)=2x-4\). Decreasing for \(x\lt 2\), increasing for \(x\gt 2\). Minimum \((2,-1)\).

  2. Find the extreme points of \(f(x)=x^3-3x\).

    Show solution

    \(f'(x)=3(x-1)(x+1)\). Maximum \((-1,2)\), minimum \((1,-2)\).

  3. Find the extreme point of \(f(x)=-x^2+6x\).

    Show solution

    \(-2x+6=0\Rightarrow x=3\). Maximum \((3,9)\).

  4. Find the extreme points of \(f(x)=x^3-12x+1\).

    Show solution

    \(3x^2-12=0\Rightarrow x=\pm2\). Maximum \((-2,17)\), minimum \((2,-15)\).

  5. Find the extreme points of \(f(x)=2x^3-3x^2\).

    Show solution

    \(f'(x)=6x^2-6x=6x(x-1)\). Maximum \((0,0)\), minimum \((1,-1)\).

  6. Find the intervals of increase and decrease of \(f(x)=x^3+3x^2\).

    Show solution

    \(f'(x)=3x(x+2)\). Increasing for \(x\lt -2\) and for \(x\gt 0\); decreasing for \(-2\lt x\lt 0\).

  7. Show that \(f(x)=x^3\) has no extreme points.

    Show solution

    \(f'(x)=3x^2\ge0\), zero only at \(x=0\). The sign of \(f'\) does not change, so \(f\) is always increasing.

  8. Find the intercepts of \(f(x)=x^3-4x\) and check whether \(f\) is even or odd.

    Show solution

    \(x(x-2)(x+2)=0\): roots \(0,\ \pm2\); y-intercept \(0\).

    \(f(-x)=-x^3+4x=-f(x)\): odd.

  9. Is \(f(x)=x^4-x^2\) even, odd, or neither?

    Show solution

    \(f(-x)=x^4-x^2=f(x)\): even.

  10. Find the extreme point of \(f(x)=x^4\).

    Show solution

    \(f'(x)=4x^3\): negative for \(x\lt 0\), positive for \(x\gt 0\). Minimum \((0,0)\).

Level B: Practice Questions 11–20

Questions with two or three steps, mixing ideas from the unit.

  1. Investigate \(f(x)=-x^3+3x^2\): intercepts, intervals of increase and decrease, extreme points and end behaviour.

    Show solution

    Roots: \(x^2(3-x)=0\Rightarrow x=0\) (double), \(x=3\).

    \(f'(x)=-3x^2+6x=-3x(x-2)\). Decreasing for \(x\lt 0\), increasing for \(0\lt x\lt 2\), decreasing for \(x\gt 2\).

    Minimum \((0,0)\), maximum \((2,4)\).

    Up on the left, down on the right.

  2. Investigate \(f(x)=x^4-8x^2\).

    Show solution

    Even. Roots: \(x^2(x^2-8)=0\Rightarrow x=0,\ \pm2\sqrt2\).

    \(f'(x)=4x(x-2)(x+2)\).

    Minimum points \((\pm2,-16)\), maximum \((0,0)\). Both ends go up.

  3. Find the largest and smallest values of \(f(x)=x^3-3x\) on \([0,2]\).

    Show solution

    Critical point inside: \(x=1\). \(f(0)=0\), \(f(1)=-2\), \(f(2)=2\).

    Largest 2, smallest \(-2\).

  4. Find the largest and smallest values of \(f(x)=x^2-6x\) on \([-1,4]\).

    Show solution

    \(f(-1)=7\), \(f(3)=-9\), \(f(4)=-8\). Largest 7, smallest \(-9\).

  5. Find the extreme points of \(f(x)=2x^3-9x^2+12x\).

    Show solution

    \(f'(x)=6x^2-18x+12=6(x-1)(x-2)\).

    Maximum \((1,5)\), minimum \((2,4)\).

  6. How many solutions does \(x^3-3x=k\) have, depending on \(k\)?

    Show solution

    Maximum value \(2\) (at \(x=-1\)), minimum value \(-2\) (at \(x=1\)).

    Three for \(-2\lt k\lt 2\); two for \(k=\pm2\); one for \(k\lt -2\) or \(k\gt 2\).

  7. For which \(a\) does \(f(x)=x^3-ax\) have an extreme point at \(x=2\)? What type is it?

    Show solution

    \(f'(2)=12-a=0\Rightarrow a=12\).

    \(f'(x)=3x^2-12\) changes from − to + at \(x=2\): a minimum, \((2,-16)\).

  8. \(f(x)=x^3+ax^2+bx\) has extreme points at \(x=1\) and \(x=3\). Find \(a\) and \(b\).

    Show solution

    \(f'(x)=3x^2+2ax+b\) has roots 1 and 3, so \(f'(x)=3(x-1)(x-3)=3x^2-12x+9\).

    \(2a=-12\Rightarrow a=-6\), \(b=9\).

  9. Show that \(f(x)=x^3+x-5\) has exactly one root, and find an interval of length 1 that contains it.

    Show solution

    \(f'(x)=3x^2+1\gt 0\): always increasing, so at most one root.

    \(f(1)=-3\lt 0\) and \(f(2)=5\gt 0\): the root is between 1 and 2.

  10. \(f(x)=x^4-4x^3\). Find the intervals of increase and decrease and the extreme points. What happens at \(x=0\)?

    Show solution

    \(f'(x)=4x^3-12x^2=4x^2(x-3)\).

    Decreasing for \(x\lt 3\), increasing for \(x\gt 3\). Minimum \((3,-27)\).

    At \(x=0\): \(f'=0\) but the sign does not change, so the tangent is horizontal and there is no extreme point.

Level C: Exam level Questions 21–30

Longer questions in the style of the matriculation (bagrut) exam.

  1. \(f(x)=x^3-3x^2-9x+11\).

    1. Find the extreme points.
    2. Show that \(x=1\) is a root and find the other roots.
    3. Describe the graph.
    4. How many solutions does \(f(x)=k\) have?
    Show solution

    (a) \(f'(x)=3(x-3)(x+1)\). Maximum \((-1,16)\), minimum \((3,-16)\).

    (b) \(f(1)=1-3-9+11=0\). Dividing: \(f(x)=(x-1)(x^2-2x-11)\), so the other roots are \(1\pm2\sqrt3\).

    (c) Increasing for \(x\lt -1\), decreasing for \(-1\lt x\lt 3\), increasing for \(x\gt 3\); y-intercept 11.

    (d) Three solutions for \(-16\lt k\lt 16\); two for \(k=\pm16\); one otherwise.

  2. \(f(x)=x^3-3x^2+k\) on the interval \([-1,3]\).

    1. Find the largest and smallest values in terms of \(k\).
    2. Find \(k\) if the largest value is 10.
    Show solution

    (a) \(f'(x)=3x(x-2)\). \(f(-1)=k-4\), \(f(0)=k\), \(f(2)=k-4\), \(f(3)=k\).

    Largest value \(k\), smallest value \(k-4\).

    (b) \(k=10\); then the smallest value is \(6\).

  3. The derivative of a function is \(f'(x)=(x-1)^2(x+2)\). Find the intervals of increase and decrease and the extreme points (x-values).

    Show solution

    \((x-1)^2\ge0\), so the sign of \(f'\) is the sign of \(x+2\).

    Decreasing for \(x\lt -2\), increasing for \(x\gt -2\). Minimum at \(x=-2\).

    At \(x=1\): \(f'=0\) without a change of sign, so no extreme point.

  4. \(f(x)=x^4-4x^3+4x^2\).

    1. Factor \(f\) and find its roots.
    2. Find the extreme points.
    3. How many solutions does \(f(x)=k\) have?
    Show solution

    (a) \(f(x)=x^2(x-2)^2\). Roots \(0\) and \(2\) (both double).

    (b) \(f'(x)=4x^3-12x^2+8x=4x(x-1)(x-2)\). Minimum \((0,0)\), maximum \((1,1)\), minimum \((2,0)\).

    (c) Four for \(0\lt k\lt 1\); three for \(k=1\); two for \(k\gt 1\) or \(k=0\); none for \(k\lt 0\).

  5. Prove that \(x^3\ge3x-2\) for every \(x\ge0\).

    Show solution

    Let \(g(x)=x^3-3x+2\). \(g'(x)=3x^2-3=0\Rightarrow x=1\) (for \(x\ge0\)).

    On \([0,\infty)\): \(g\) decreases until \(x=1\) and then increases, so its smallest value is \(g(1)=0\).

    So \(g(x)\ge0\), that is \(x^3\ge3x-2\). (Indeed \(g(x)=(x-1)^2(x+2)\).)

  6. Find a polynomial of degree 3 with a maximum at \((0,4)\) and a minimum at \((2,0)\).

    Show solution

    \(f(x)=ax^3+bx^2+cx+d\). \(f(0)=4\Rightarrow d=4\); \(f'(0)=0\Rightarrow c=0\).

    \(f'(2)=12a+4b=0\Rightarrow b=-3a\). \(f(2)=8a+4b+4=0\Rightarrow -4a+4=0\Rightarrow a=1,\ b=-3\).

    \(f(x)=x^3-3x^2+4\). Check: \(f'(x)=3x(x-2)\), signs + − +: max at 0, min at 2 ✓

  7. \(f(x)=x^3-3x\) on the interval \([-2,a]\) with \(a\gt -1\). For which values of \(a\) is the largest value reached at the right end \(x=a\)?

    Show solution

    Inside, the maximum point is \((-1,2)\), and \(f(-2)=-2\). We need \(f(a)\ge2\):

    \(a^3-3a-2\ge0\Rightarrow (a-2)(a+1)^2\ge0\Rightarrow a\ge2\).

    For \(a\ge2\) (at \(a=2\) the value 2 is reached at both \(x=-1\) and \(x=2\)).

  8. A body moves along a line. Its position after \(t\) seconds is \(s(t)=t^3-9t^2+24t\) metres (\(t\ge0\)).

    1. Find the velocity \(v(t)=s'(t)\).
    2. When does the body move forward and when backward?
    3. Find the total distance travelled in the first 5 seconds.
    Show solution

    (a) \(v(t)=s'(t)=3t^2-18t+24=3(t-2)(t-4)\).

    (b) Forward for \(0\lt t\lt 2\) and \(t\gt 4\); backward for \(2\lt t\lt 4\).

    (c) \(s(0)=0\), \(s(2)=20\), \(s(4)=16\), \(s(5)=20\). Distance \(=20+4+4=28\) m.

  9. \(f(x)=x^3+px\). Show that for \(p\gt 0\) the function has no extreme points, and for \(p\lt 0\) it has two.

    Show solution

    \(f'(x)=3x^2+p\).

    \(p\gt 0\): \(f'\gt 0\) always, so \(f\) increases everywhere.

    \(p\lt 0\): \(f'(x)=0\Rightarrow x=\pm\sqrt{-p/3}\), and \(f'\) changes sign at both: a maximum and a minimum.

  10. For which values of \(c\) does \(x^4-4x+c=0\) have two solutions? One? None?

    Show solution

    Let \(f(x)=x^4-4x\). \(f'(x)=4x^3-4=0\Rightarrow x=1\): a minimum, \(f(1)=-3\). Both ends go up.

    The equation is \(f(x)=-c\).

    Two solutions: \(-c\gt -3\Rightarrow c\lt 3\). One: \(c=3\). None: \(c\gt 3\).
