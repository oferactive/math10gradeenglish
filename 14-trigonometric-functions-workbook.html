Level A: Basic Questions 1–10

Use the definitions and formulas directly.

  1. Convert to radians.

    1. 90°
    2. 60°
    3. 270°
    4. 45°
    Show solution

    (a) \(\tfrac\pi2\)

    (b) \(\tfrac\pi3\)

    (c) \(\tfrac{3\pi}2\)

    (d) \(\tfrac\pi4\)

  2. Convert to degrees.

    1. \(\tfrac\pi6\)
    2. \(\tfrac{2\pi}3\)
    3. \(\tfrac{5\pi}4\)
    4. \(3\pi\)
    Show solution

    (a) 30°

    (b) 120°

    (c) 225°

    (d) 540°

  3. Write the point \(P(t)\) and the values \(\sin t\), \(\cos t\) for \(t=\tfrac\pi2,\ \pi,\ \tfrac{3\pi}2,\ 2\pi\).

    Show solution

    \(\tfrac\pi2\): \((0,1)\), \(\sin=1,\cos=0\).

    \(\pi\): \((-1,0)\), \(\sin=0,\cos=-1\).

    \(\tfrac{3\pi}2\): \((0,-1)\), \(\sin=-1,\cos=0\).

    \(2\pi\): \((1,0)\), \(\sin=0,\cos=1\).

  4. Write the exact value.

    1. \(\sin\tfrac\pi6\)
    2. \(\cos\tfrac\pi3\)
    3. \(\tan\tfrac\pi4\)
    4. \(\sin\tfrac\pi3\)
    Show solution

    (a) \(\tfrac12\)

    (b) \(\tfrac12\)

    (c) \(1\)

    (d) \(\tfrac{\sqrt3}2\)

  5. Find the signs of \(\sin t\) and \(\cos t\).

    1. \(t=2\)
    2. \(t=4\)
    3. \(t=5\)
    Show solution

    (a) \(2\) is between \(\tfrac\pi2\approx1.57\) and \(\pi\approx3.14\): \(\sin\gt 0\), \(\cos\lt 0\).

    (b) \(4\) is between \(\pi\) and \(\tfrac{3\pi}2\approx4.71\): both negative.

    (c) \(5\) is between \(\tfrac{3\pi}2\) and \(2\pi\approx6.28\): \(\sin\lt 0\), \(\cos\gt 0\).

  6. Use symmetry to find the exact value.

    1. \(\sin\left(-\tfrac\pi6\right)\)
    2. \(\cos\left(-\tfrac\pi3\right)\)
    3. \(\sin\tfrac{5\pi}6\)
    4. \(\cos\tfrac{2\pi}3\)
    Show solution

    (a) \(-\tfrac12\)

    (b) \(\tfrac12\)

    (c) \(\tfrac12\)

    (d) \(-\tfrac12\)

  7. \(\sin t=0.6\) and \(0\lt t\lt \tfrac\pi2\). Find \(\cos t\) and \(\tan t\).

    Show solution

    \(\cos t=\sqrt{1-0.36}=0.8\); \(\tan t=\dfrac{0.6}{0.8}=0.75\).

  8. Use periodicity to find the exact value.

    1. \(\sin\tfrac{13\pi}6\)
    2. \(\cos\tfrac{9\pi}4\)
    Show solution

    (a) \(\sin\left(2\pi+\tfrac\pi6\right)=\tfrac12\)

    (b) \(\cos\left(2\pi+\tfrac\pi4\right)=\tfrac{\sqrt2}2\)

  9. Find the amplitude and the period.

    1. \(y=3\sin x\)
    2. \(y=\sin 2x\)
    3. \(y=\cos\tfrac x2\)
    Show solution

    (a) amplitude 3, period \(2\pi\)

    (b) amplitude 1, period \(\pi\)

    (c) amplitude 1, period \(4\pi\)

  10. A right triangle has legs 3 and 4 and hypotenuse 5. For the angle \(\alpha\) opposite the side 3, find \(\sin\alpha\), \(\cos\alpha\) and \(\tan\alpha\).

    Show solution

    \(\sin\alpha=\tfrac35\), \(\cos\alpha=\tfrac45\), \(\tan\alpha=\tfrac34\).

Level B: Practice Questions 11–20

Questions with two or three steps, mixing ideas from the unit.

  1. \(\cos t=-\tfrac5{13}\) and \(\tfrac\pi2\lt t\lt \pi\). Find \(\sin t\) and \(\tan t\).

    Show solution

    \(\sin t=\tfrac{12}{13}\), \(\tan t=-\tfrac{12}5\).

  2. \(\tan t=2\) and \(\pi\lt t\lt \tfrac{3\pi}2\). Find \(\sin t\) and \(\cos t\).

    Show solution

    \(\sin t=2\cos t\Rightarrow 4\cos^2t+\cos^2t=1\Rightarrow\cos^2t=\tfrac15\).

    In the third quarter both are negative: \(\cos t=-\tfrac1{\sqrt5}\), \(\sin t=-\tfrac2{\sqrt5}\).

  3. Simplify \(\sin^2x+\cos^2x+\tan x\cos x\).

    Show solution

    \(1+\dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}\cdot\cos x=1+\sin x\)

  4. Prove that \((\sin x+\cos x)^2=1+2\sin x\cos x\).

    Show solution

    \(\sin^2x+2\sin x\cos x+\cos^2x=1+2\sin x\cos x\)

  5. Find the exact value.

    1. \(\sin\tfrac{7\pi}6\)
    2. \(\cos\tfrac{5\pi}3\)
    3. \(\tan\tfrac{3\pi}4\)
    4. \(\sin\left(-\tfrac{3\pi}2\right)\)
    Show solution

    (a) \(-\tfrac12\)

    (b) \(\tfrac12\)

    (c) \(-1\)

    (d) \(1\)

  6. Find the range of \(y=2\sin x+1\) and the values of \(x\) where it is largest.

    Show solution

    \(-1\le y\le3\). Largest when \(\sin x=1\): \(x=\tfrac\pi2+2\pi k\).

  7. How is \(y=\sin\left(x-\tfrac\pi3\right)\) obtained from \(y=\sin x\)? Find its zeros.

    Show solution

    Shift \(\tfrac\pi3\) to the right. Zeros: \(x-\tfrac\pi3=k\pi\Rightarrow x=\tfrac\pi3+k\pi\).

  8. Find the range of \(y=1-\cos x\) and where it is smallest.

    Show solution

    \(0\le y\le2\). Smallest (0) when \(\cos x=1\): \(x=2\pi k\).

  9. Explain, using the unit circle and similar triangles, why \(\tan t=\dfrac{\sin t}{\cos t}\) for \(0\lt t\lt \tfrac\pi2\).

    Show solution

    Triangles \(OQP\) (legs \(\cos t,\sin t\)) and \(OAT\) (legs \(1,\tan t\)) share the angle \(t\) at \(O\) and have a right angle, so they are similar: \(\dfrac{\tan t}1=\dfrac{\sin t}{\cos t}\).

  10. Simplify.

    1. \(\sin(\pi-x)+\cos\left(\tfrac\pi2-x\right)\)
    2. \(\cos(-x)-\cos x\)
    Show solution

    (a) \(\sin x+\sin x=2\sin x\)

    (b) \(\cos x-\cos x=0\)

Level C: Exam level Questions 21–30

Longer questions in the style of the matriculation (bagrut) exam.

  1. Prove that \(1+\tan^2x=\dfrac1{\cos^2x}\). Then find \(\tan^2x\) if \(\cos x=\tfrac13\).

    Show solution

    \(1+\dfrac{\sin^2x}{\cos^2x}=\dfrac{\cos^2x+\sin^2x}{\cos^2x}=\dfrac1{\cos^2x}\).

    \(\tan^2x=9-1=8\).

  2. \(\sin x+\cos x=1.2\). Find \(\sin x\cos x\).

    Show solution

    Square: \(1+2\sin x\cos x=1.44\Rightarrow\sin x\cos x=0.22\).

  3. The height of a seat on a Ferris wheel after \(t\) minutes is \(h(t)=10-8\cos\left(\tfrac{\pi t}{15}\right)\) metres.

    1. Find the lowest and highest heights.
    2. How long does one turn take?
    3. Find the height after 10 minutes.
    Show solution

    (a) Lowest \(2\) m (at \(t=0\)), highest \(18\) m.

    (b) Period \(\dfrac{2\pi}{\pi/15}=30\) minutes.

    (c) \(h(10)=10-8\cos\tfrac{2\pi}3=10+4=14\) m.

  4. In a certain city the number of daylight hours on day \(t\) of the year is about \(D(t)=12+2.5\sin\left(\dfrac{2\pi(t-80)}{365}\right)\).

    1. Find the longest and the shortest day.
    2. On which days is \(D=12\)?
    Show solution

    (a) Longest 14.5 hours, shortest 9.5 hours.

    (b) \(D=12\) when the sine is 0: \(t=80\) (around 21 March) and half a period later, \(t\approx262\) (around 19 September).

  5. Find the largest and smallest values of \(f(x)=3-2\sin^2x\), and write \(f\) using \(\cos x\) only.

    Show solution

    \(0\le\sin^2x\le1\Rightarrow 1\le f(x)\le3\).

    \(f(x)=3-2(1-\cos^2x)=1+2\cos^2x\).

  6. For which \(t\) in \([0,2\pi]\) does the point \(P(t)\) lie on the line \(y=x\)?

    Show solution

    \(\sin t=\cos t\Rightarrow\tan t=1\Rightarrow t=\tfrac\pi4\) or \(t=\tfrac{5\pi}4\).

  7. \(A(1,0)\) and \(P(t)\) is on the unit circle, \(0\lt t\lt \pi\). Find the area of triangle \(OAP\) as a function of \(t\). When is it largest?

    Show solution

    Base \(OA=1\), height \(\sin t\): area \(=\tfrac12\sin t\).

    Largest when \(t=\tfrac\pi2\): area \(\tfrac12\).

  8. Explain with the unit circle why \(\sin\left(\tfrac\pi2-t\right)=\cos t\).

    Show solution

    Reflecting the circle in the line \(y=x\) sends \(P(t)=(\cos t,\sin t)\) to \((\sin t,\cos t)\).

    The reflected point makes the angle \(\tfrac\pi2-t\) with the x-axis, so it is \(P\left(\tfrac\pi2-t\right)\), whose y-coordinate is \(\cos t\).

  9. Which is larger, \(\sin1\) or \(\sin2\)? Answer without a calculator.

    Show solution

    \(\sin2=\sin(\pi-2)\approx\sin1.14\).

    On \(\left[0,\tfrac\pi2\right]\) sine increases, and \(1.14\gt 1\), so \(\sin2\gt \sin1\).

  10. \(y=2\sin(2x)-1\).

    1. Find the amplitude, period and middle line.
    2. Find the range.
    3. Find \(y(0)\) and the first maximum point for \(x\gt 0\).
    4. Find the zeros.
    Show solution

    (a) Amplitude 2, period \(\pi\), middle line \(y=-1\).

    (b) \(-3\le y\le1\).

    (c) \(y(0)=-1\); largest value 1 at \(x=\tfrac\pi4\).

    (d) \(\sin2x=\tfrac12\Rightarrow 2x=\tfrac\pi6\) or \(\tfrac{5\pi}6\) (plus \(2\pi k\)) \(\Rightarrow x=\tfrac\pi{12}+\pi k\) or \(x=\tfrac{5\pi}{12}+\pi k\).
