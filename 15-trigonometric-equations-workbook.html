Workbook: Trigonometric Equations

30 questions in three levels, each with a full worked solution. Learn by doing: solve on paper first, then open the solution and compare your method. The explanation is on the page Trigonometric Equations.

Before you start: For every equation, first find one angle \(\alpha\), then write the full general solution, and only then pick the values in the interval. Check one answer with the calculator.

Level A: Basic Questions 1–10

Use the definitions and formulas directly.

  1. Solve.

    1. \(\sin x=0\)
    2. \(\cos x=0\)
    3. \(\tan x=0\)
    Show solution

    (a) \(x=\pi k\)

    (b) \(x=\tfrac\pi2+\pi k\)

    (c) \(x=\pi k\)

  2. Solve.

    1. \(\sin x=1\)
    2. \(\cos x=-1\)
    Show solution

    (a) \(x=\tfrac\pi2+2\pi k\)

    (b) \(x=\pi+2\pi k\)

  3. Solve \(\sin x=\tfrac{\sqrt2}2\).

    Show solution

    \(x=\tfrac\pi4+2\pi k\) or \(x=\tfrac{3\pi}4+2\pi k\).

  4. Solve \(\cos x=\tfrac12\).

    Show solution

    \(x=\pm\tfrac\pi3+2\pi k\).

  5. Solve \(\tan x=1\).

    Show solution

    \(x=\tfrac\pi4+\pi k\).

  6. Solve \(\sin x=-\tfrac12\).

    Show solution

    \(x=-\tfrac\pi6+2\pi k\) or \(x=\tfrac{7\pi}6+2\pi k\).

  7. Solve \(\cos x=-\tfrac{\sqrt3}2\).

    Show solution

    \(x=\pm\tfrac{5\pi}6+2\pi k\).

  8. Solve \(\tan x=-\sqrt3\).

    Show solution

    \(x=-\tfrac\pi3+\pi k\).

  9. Which of these equations have solutions?

    1. \(\sin x=1.5\)
    2. \(\cos x=-2\)
    3. \(\tan x=100\)
    Show solution

    (a) no, \(1.5\gt 1\)

    (b) no, \(-2\lt -1\)

    (c) yes: tangent takes every value

  10. Solve \(\sin x=\tfrac12\) in \([0,2\pi]\).

    Show solution

    \(\tfrac\pi6\) and \(\tfrac{5\pi}6\).

Level B: Practice Questions 11–20

Questions with two or three steps, mixing ideas from the unit.

  1. Solve \(2\cos x-1=0\) in \([0,2\pi]\).

    Show solution

    \(\cos x=\tfrac12\): \(x=\tfrac\pi3\) and \(x=\tfrac{5\pi}3\).

  2. Solve \(2\sin\left(x-\tfrac\pi4\right)+1=0\).

    Show solution

    \(x=\tfrac\pi{12}+2\pi k\) or \(x=\tfrac{17\pi}{12}+2\pi k\).

  3. Solve \(\sin 2x=\tfrac{\sqrt3}2\) in \([0,2\pi]\).

    Show solution

    \(\tfrac\pi6,\ \tfrac\pi3,\ \tfrac{7\pi}6,\ \tfrac{4\pi}3\).

  4. Solve \(\cos 3x=0\).

    Show solution

    \(3x=\tfrac\pi2+\pi k\Rightarrow x=\tfrac\pi6+\tfrac{\pi k}3\).

  5. Solve \(\tan\left(x+\tfrac\pi6\right)=1\).

    Show solution

    \(x+\tfrac\pi6=\tfrac\pi4+\pi k\Rightarrow x=\tfrac\pi{12}+\pi k\).

  6. Solve \(\sin x=0.3\) in \([0,2\pi]\) with a calculator (3 decimal places).

    Show solution

    \(\sin^{-1}0.3\approx0.305\). Solutions \(0.305\) and \(\pi-0.305\approx2.837\).

  7. Solve \(\cos x=-0.4\) in \([0,2\pi]\).

    Show solution

    \(\cos^{-1}(-0.4)\approx1.982\). Solutions \(1.982\) and \(2\pi-1.982\approx4.301\).

  8. Solve \(4\sin^2x=3\).

    Show solution

    \(\sin x=\pm\tfrac{\sqrt3}2\Rightarrow x=\pm\tfrac\pi3+\pi k\).

  9. Solve \(\sin x=\cos x\).

    Show solution

    \(\cos x\ne0\) here (otherwise \(\sin x=\pm1\ne0\)). Divide: \(\tan x=1\Rightarrow x=\tfrac\pi4+\pi k\).

  10. How many solutions does \(\sin x=0.4\) have in \([0,4\pi]\)? Explain with a sketch.

    Show solution

    Two in each period of length \(2\pi\): four solutions.

Level C: Exam level Questions 21–30

Longer questions in the style of the matriculation (bagrut) exam.

  1. Solve \(2\sin^2x-\sin x-1=0\).

    Show solution

    \((2\sin x+1)(\sin x-1)=0\).

    \(x=\tfrac\pi2+2\pi k\), or \(x=-\tfrac\pi6+2\pi k\), or \(x=\tfrac{7\pi}6+2\pi k\).

  2. Solve \(2\cos^2x+3\sin x-3=0\).

    Show solution

    \(2(1-\sin^2x)+3\sin x-3=0\Rightarrow 2\sin^2x-3\sin x+1=0\Rightarrow(2\sin x-1)(\sin x-1)=0\).

    \(x=\tfrac\pi2+2\pi k\), \(x=\tfrac\pi6+2\pi k\), \(x=\tfrac{5\pi}6+2\pi k\).

  3. Solve \(\tan x=2\sin x\).

    Show solution

    \(\dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}=2\sin x\Rightarrow\sin x\left(\dfrac1{\cos x}-2\right)=0\).

    \(\sin x=0\Rightarrow x=\pi k\); \(\cos x=\tfrac12\Rightarrow x=\pm\tfrac\pi3+2\pi k\).

  4. A Ferris wheel seat is at height \(h(t)=10-8\cos\left(\tfrac{\pi t}{15}\right)\) metres after \(t\) minutes.

    1. When during the first turn is the seat at 14 m?
    2. For how long in each turn is it higher than 14 m?
    3. When is it at its lowest point?
    Show solution

    (a) \(\cos\left(\tfrac{\pi t}{15}\right)=-\tfrac12\Rightarrow t=10\) or \(t=20\).

    (b) From minute 10 to minute 20: 10 minutes out of each 30-minute turn.

    (c) \(h=2\) only when \(\cos=1\): \(t=0,30,60,\dots\)

  5. Find the points where the graphs of \(y=\sin x\) and \(y=\cos x\) meet in \([0,2\pi]\).

    Show solution

    \(\tan x=1\Rightarrow x=\tfrac\pi4,\ \tfrac{5\pi}4\).

    Points \(\left(\tfrac\pi4,\tfrac{\sqrt2}2\right)\) and \(\left(\tfrac{5\pi}4,-\tfrac{\sqrt2}2\right)\).

  6. Use the rule "\(\sin A=\sin B\) when \(A=B+2\pi k\) or \(A=\pi-B+2\pi k\)" to solve \(\sin 2x=\sin x\).

    Show solution

    \(2x=x+2\pi k\Rightarrow x=2\pi k\).

    \(2x=\pi-x+2\pi k\Rightarrow x=\tfrac\pi3+\tfrac{2\pi k}3\).

  7. Solve \(\cos\left(x+\tfrac\pi3\right)=\cos x\), using "\(\cos A=\cos B\) when \(A=\pm B+2\pi k\)".

    Show solution

    \(x+\tfrac\pi3=x+2\pi k\) is impossible.

    \(x+\tfrac\pi3=-x+2\pi k\Rightarrow x=-\tfrac\pi6+\pi k\).

  8. Solve \(|\sin x|=\tfrac12\) in \([0,2\pi]\).

    Show solution

    \(\sin x=\pm\tfrac12\): \(\tfrac\pi6,\ \tfrac{5\pi}6,\ \tfrac{7\pi}6,\ \tfrac{11\pi}6\).

  9. For which values of \(a\) does \(\sin x=a\) have exactly three solutions in \([0,2\pi]\)? Exactly one? Exactly two?

    Show solution

    Three: \(a=0\) (\(0,\pi,2\pi\)). One: \(a=\pm1\). Two: \(0\lt |a|\lt 1\). None: \(|a|\gt 1\).

  10. Daylight hours: \(D(t)=12+2.5\sin\left(\dfrac{2\pi(t-80)}{365}\right)\), \(t\) in days. On which days is \(D=13.25\)? For how many days a year is the day longer than 13.25 hours?

    Show solution

    \(\sin(\dots)=0.5\Rightarrow\dfrac{2\pi(t-80)}{365}=\tfrac\pi6\) or \(\tfrac{5\pi}6\).

    \(t-80\approx30.4\) or \(152.1\Rightarrow t\approx110\) and \(t\approx232\).

    Longer than 13.25 hours for about \(232-110=122\) days.
