Mathematics for Grade 10

Explanation and practice pages in mathematics for Grade 10 students at the 5-unit level, following the Israeli syllabus. Each page explains one topic in depth: what it means, where the formulas come from, how to solve problems and the common mistakes, followed by graded practice questions with full solutions.

Contents

  1. Curriculum

Two pages per unit

Each unit has an explanation page (ideas, formulas, worked examples and short "check yourself" exercises) and a workbook with 30 questions in three levels: basic, practice and exam level.

How to practise

Solutions are hidden. Solve on your own first, then click "Show solution" to check your method and answer.

Suggested order

Read the explanation first, then work through the workbook. Units are numbered in the suggested teaching order; within each subject area each unit builds on the ones before it.
